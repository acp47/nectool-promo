## Video Direction

**Tone:** Professional, calm, human-centered.

**Color Language:** Nectool brand blues (#00051B, #326CDE) on clean white backgrounds (#FFFFFF). Accent pops for wellness elements (greens, warm tones from capture).

**Typography:** Montserrat Variable for both display and body (bold for headlines, regular for supporting text). Generous spacing; no cramped layouts.

**Motion Doctrine:** All motion is intentional and performance-driven. No idle breathing. Micro-interactions stagger to guide eye through story. Transitions are smooth fades or cuts; no jarring pops.

**Asset Integration:** Real screenshots from nectool.dk give authenticity. Wellness imagery (meditation, exercises) shows tangible value. Diverse personnel imagery emphasizes inclusivity for employees and managers.

**Music:** Calm ambient track, no percussion. Supports narrative but never dominates. Fades in at Frame 1 (0s), holds through Frames 1–2 (13s), gently fades out during Frame 3 CTA (22–30s).
