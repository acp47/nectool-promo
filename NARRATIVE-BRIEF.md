# Nectool Narrative Brief — Pain → Situation → Complexity → Solution

## Why We Built Nectool

**Mission:** Build a platform to bring expert knowledge and actionable advice to workers struggling with workplace stress, interpersonal friction, and burnout.

---

## Story Arc (30 seconds)

### **Act 1: The Pain (0-5s)**
**Emotional Truth:** Recognition of workplace struggle

**Problem Statement:**
- Endless meetings
- Tight deadlines
- Struggling with colleagues
- Feeling stuck, frustrated, isolated

**Visual:** Full-screen hero image showing workplace conflict/stress
**Text:** Animated captions with emphasis on pain points
- "Endless meetings. Tight deadlines. **Struggling** with colleagues."

**Tone:** Empathetic — we recognize your struggle

---

### **Act 2: The Complexity → Solution (5-13s)**
**Emotional Truth:** The solution exists, but knowledge is locked away

**The Situation:**
- Expert knowledge exists in the world
- Psychology, communication, team dynamics
- But it's scattered, expensive, or locked in PDFs

**The Complexity:**
- People KNOW they need help
- But expert knowledge isn't accessible
- It feels out of reach — impractical, expensive, time-consuming
- Even when available, it's not actionable

**The Solution:**
- Nectool brings expert knowledge into a **simple, free, actionable platform**
- Real courses on mental reactions, conflict handling, team building
- Practical exercises, not theory
- Available to everyone — restaurants, media, agriculture, offices, any workplace

**Visual:** Cross-fading course screenshots showing real lessons & exercises
**Text:** Overlay on hero images
- "Expert Knowledge. Actionable Advice. Finally within reach."

**Tone:** Hopeful — the solution is here, it's accessible, it's for you

---

### **Act 3: The Solution + Call to Action (13-30s)**
**Emotional Truth:** The solution is real, available, and waiting for you

**The Solution in Action:**
- **Nectool is Here** — the platform exists, it's real
- **Free** — no paywall, no corporate gatekeeping
- **Simple** — easy to use, not overwhelming
- **For Everyone** — restaurants, media, agriculture, offices, any workplace
- **Proven** — real courses that help people improve mental reactions, handle conflicts, build stronger teams

**The Call to Action:**
- **Start today** — begin your journey now
- **See what happens tomorrow** — results come quickly with consistent practice

**Visual:** Full-screen hero showing Nectool platform/teams interface
**Text Overlay:**
- Message: "Nectool is Here. Free. Simple. For restaurants, media, agriculture, offices. Any workplace."
- CTA Button: "Start for Free"
- URL: nectool.dk

**Tone:** Motivational — this is your moment to change your workplace culture

---

## Key Narrative Principles

1. **Empathy First:** Start by recognizing the real pain people feel (not lecturing about solutions)
2. **Concrete Evidence:** Show actual course screenshots, not generic stock footage
3. **Accessibility:** Emphasize "free," "simple," "for everyone"
4. **Hope:** Transform despair (Act 1) → possibility (Act 2) → action (Act 3)
5. **Nectool Prominence:** Brand appears in every frame as the central solution

---

## Tone & Voice

- **Empathetic:** Validates the struggle without minimizing it
- **Motivational:** Inspires belief that change is possible
- **Practical:** Focuses on actionable next steps, not abstract concepts
- **Inclusive:** Speaks to any workplace, any role, any person struggling

1. **Recognize themselves** in Act 1 (emotional connection)
